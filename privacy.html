Privacy Policy

MN Fair Foodie Finder · Last updated August 22, 2026

MN Fair Foodie Finder ("the app") helps you find, rate, and share Minnesota State Fair food. This policy explains what we collect and how it's used.

What we collect

  • Account info: if you create an account, we store your email address and the profile you set up (display name, handle, avatar, bio).
  • Content you create: reviews, pup ratings, photos you attach, food lists, check-ins, and comments. Public content (public lists, reviews, your passport level) is visible to other users.
  • Usage on your device: your lists, check-ins, and preferences are stored locally on your device, and synced to your account when you're signed in.
  • Share stats: when a shared list link is opened, we record anonymous view/save counts for the list's creator.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell your personal information.
  • We don't show third-party ads or share your data with advertisers.
  • We don't track your precise location — the fairgrounds map uses fixed vendor locations, not your GPS.

Where data lives

Account data and shared content are stored with Supabase (our database provider) in the United States. Sign-in with Google or Apple is handled by those providers; we receive only your email address and basic profile info.

Your choices

  • You can edit your profile, delete your reviews and lists, and block other users at any time in the app.
  • To delete your account and its data, contact us at the email below and we'll remove it.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from them.

Contact

Questions or data requests: support@mnfairfoodieguide.com

We may update this policy as the app evolves; material changes will be noted in the app.
