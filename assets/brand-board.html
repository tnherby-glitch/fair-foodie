MN FAIR

Foodie Finder

The concept: the Pin-Pup. The prontopup and the map pin share a silhouette — a rounded body tapering to a point. One shape says both "fair food" and "find it." The drizzle traces a subtle M for Minnesota, tilted four degrees so it reads as sauce, not a sticker. Warm batter gold, ketchup red, and a blue-ribbon blue reserved for the best of the best.

05 · Finish Notes
  • White does the work. Paper canvas, 24px gutters, cards breathe. Color appears in food photography, the gold CTA, and the rating row — almost nowhere else.
  • Photography is the hero. Full-bleed tiles, 20px radius, natural light, food filling the frame. No filters that fight the batter-gold palette.
  • Rate in prontopups. The five-pup rating row is the signature interaction. Filled pups in gold, empties in warm gray outline. Tapping a pup gives a quick 1.1x spring.
  • Blue Ribbon is earned. The ribbon badge appears only on foods rated 4.8+ with 100+ ratings. Scarcity keeps it meaningful — never use ribbon blue decoratively.
  • Motion is a garnish. 200–250ms ease-out on cards, a single spring on rating taps, skeleton shimmer while photos load. Nothing loops, nothing bounces twice.
  • Depth is soft. One shadow token at rest, one on lift. No borders on photo cards; hairlines only on utility surfaces.
